Steak Frites in Jakarta: Why BRIQ Bistro Is the Home of the Steak Frites in Senopati

5 November 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

A properly seared steak, crisp frites and a sauce worth mopping up, with beef from our own butcher, Brock.

Jakarta, 5 November 2026 — Ask anyone who has eaten at BRIQ Bistro what to order and the answer is the same: the steak frites. It is the dish BRIQ is known for, and the reason we call ourselves the home of the steak frites in Senopati.

It starts with the beef

Most restaurants buy their steak from a supplier. BRIQ buys it from Brock Butchery, the butcher we run ourselves. That means we choose the cuts, we set the spec, and if the quality ever slips, we are the first to know. It is the same beef Brock delivers to home kitchens across Jakarta.

Steak frites, the BRIQ way

  • The steak: seared hard and rested properly, so it is cooked the way you asked for it.
  • The sauce: Café de Paris butter or sauce au poivre.
  • The frites: crisp outside, soft inside, and plenty of them.

Steak frites for two

The BRIQ Date Package runs every day, all night: two ribeye steaks with your choice of Café de Paris butter or sauce au poivre, and two portions of fries, for 495K++ for two.

A French restaurant in Senopati

BRIQ is a French bistro crafted between fine and casual: the care of fine dining, with the warmth of a neighbourhood restaurant. Dinner runs Monday to Friday from 17:00, and at weekends we are open all day, from 10:00 to 22:00.

At weekend lunchtimes there is also our weekend pasta promo: ravioli, rigatoni or tagliatelle with a salad and a coffee, tea or soft drink for 200K++, from 11:30 to 15:00.
