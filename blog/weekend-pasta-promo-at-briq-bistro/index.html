Weekend Pasta at BRIQ: Ravioli, Rigatoni or Tagliatelle, with Salad and a Drink, for 200K

3 November 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

Make your weekends a little more special: pasta, a salad and a drink, every Saturday and Sunday lunchtime.

Jakarta, 3 November 2026 — BRIQ Bistro has launched its weekend Pasta Promo, a new reason to spend a slow Saturday or Sunday lunch in Senopati. Pick your pasta, add a salad and a drink, and the whole thing comes to 200K++.

How it works

  • Choose your pasta: ravioli, rigatoni or tagliatelle.
  • Add your choice of salad.
  • And a drink: coffee, tea or a soft drink.
  • 200K++, every weekend from 11:30 to 15:00.

Weekends at BRIQ, all day

On weekdays BRIQ is a dinner spot, but on Saturdays and Sundays we’re open all day, from 10:00 to 22:00. Pasta at lunch, a glass of something on the terrace in the afternoon, and dinner if you’re not ready to leave.

BRIQ is still the home of the steak frites: a properly seared steak, crisp frites and a sauce worth mopping up, made with beef from our own butcher, Brock.

Bringing someone?

The BRIQ Date Package runs every day, all night: two ribeye steaks with your choice of Café de Paris butter or sauce au poivre, and two portions of fries, for 495K++ for two. Add escargots and two drinks for 100K++.

BRIQ Bistro

A neighbourhood French bistro in Senopati, crafted between fine and casual. Dinner Monday to Friday from 17:00; all day at weekends.

Jl. Prof. Joko Sutono SH No. 7B, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta
