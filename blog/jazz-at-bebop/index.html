Jazz at BEBOP Creates Moments That Don’t Happen Twice

15 September 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

Each week, a live jazz night you don’t want to miss.

Jakarta, 15 September 2026 — What started as a weekly fixture for the city’s music lovers has grown into one of Jakarta’s go-to stages for jazz that truly feels alive.

Curated by Bass G

The programme at BEBOP is curated by Bass G, resident jazz curator and musician, who oversees the line-ups and musical direction. Each night shows a different side of jazz — from traditional playing to funk, groove and more modern sounds — unpredictable enough to keep the audience coming back for a different vibe each time, and never less than a good time.

“We want musicians to have room to be themselves and interact with the audience. The programme tries to show that jazz can be energetic, fun, and accessible, rather than something that has to feel distant or overly serious.”

Bass G, resident jazz curator

Jazz at BEBOP is rooted in the belief that it is not just about playing great music, but about creating an experience where the audience feels involved. Come to listen attentively, or have a drink, talk, and enjoy the music without needing to understand jazz beforehand.

Big names, small room

Over the past year, BEBOP’s stage has hosted some of Indonesia’s most respected names in jazz and beyond, including Dewa Budjana, Barry Likumahuwa and Sandhy Sandoro — a line-up that reflects BEBOP’s commitment to bringing serious musicianship into an intimate, unpretentious setting.

When to go

Jazz nights run every Monday and Friday at BEBOP Bar + Kitchen, Jl. Senopati No. 61, featuring a rotating cast of local musicians alongside occasional special guests.
