What Lies Beneath the Night: A Look Back at Halloween Week at BEBOP

3 November 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

Six nights, a lot of costumes and one very full dance floor. Thank you for dancing beneath the shadows with us.

Jakarta, 3 November 2026 — For one week at the end of October, BEBOP Bar + Kitchen turned into something a little stranger than usual. What Lies Beneath the Night, our Halloween Week, ran from Monday 26 to Saturday 31 October, with a different night every night and a costume dress code for all of it.

Six nights, six moods

  • Monday 26 Oct — Special Monday Jazz. Our weekly live jazz night, with a darker set list.
  • Tuesday 27 Oct — Trivia Night with Kara Chenoa. Brains, banter and bonus points for costumes.
  • Wednesday 28 Oct — Lady Gaga Listening Party. Played front to back, the way a listening bar should.
  • Thursday 29 Oct — Spooky Karaoke with cryonbike. Less about hitting the note, more about bringing the house down.
  • Friday 30 Oct — Booty Groove with Jaka, Yelig (Club Odyssey) and Laskar.
  • Saturday 31 Oct — Bebop Music Collective on the decks to close the week.

Why we do weeks like this

BEBOP has always been more than a bar in Senopati: it’s a room built around music and the people who come back for it. Halloween Week brought together everything we run through the year, from live jazz and trivia to karaoke and the Bebop Music Collective, in one six-night run. Thank you to everyone who dressed up, sang, danced and stayed late.

What’s next at BEBOP

  • Happy hour, every night 5–8 PM: selected cocktails for 100K.
  • Live jazz every Monday and Friday — still one of the best answers to “jazz bar in Jakarta”. Recently, Between the Notes with Bass G brought Barry Likumahuwa to our stage.
  • Trivia Tuesdays, karaoke and BMC nights through the week. Follow @bebop.wav for the line-up.
