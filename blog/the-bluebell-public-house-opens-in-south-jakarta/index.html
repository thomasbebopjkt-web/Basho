The Bluebell Public House Opens in South Jakarta — Bringing the Warmth of a British Pub to Senopati

5 October 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

Raise a pint, British-style, at the newest pub in the heart of South Jakarta.

Jakarta, 3 October 2026 — The Bluebell Public House introduces a refined yet deeply welcoming slice of British pub culture to the heart of Senopati. Designed as a dimly lit, warm abode, the space pairs an elevated-casual dining experience with a curated drinks programme, making it the perfect setting for post-work unwinding and intimate catch-ups.

The concept captures the social soul of the traditional public house, offering cosy nooks where daytime conversations naturally stretch into long, relaxed evenings.

The menu strikes a balance between comfort and sophistication, featuring thoughtfully elevated British classics alongside draught beers, fine spirits, and signature cocktails. Approachably premium and easy to enjoy, the food and drinks are crafted for any occasion — whether you are stopping by for a quick bite after office hours or settling in for a nightcap.

Tucked into Senopati’s vibrant landscape, The Bluebell Public House offers a distinct alternative to the neighbourhood’s bustling dining scene, blending the timeless familiarity of pub culture into a contemporary Jakarta setting.

About The Bluebell Public House

The Bluebell Public House is a British-inspired pub in Senopati, South Jakarta. The concept brings together food, drinks, and a relaxed social atmosphere, drawing on the character of the British pub while making it its own in Jakarta.
