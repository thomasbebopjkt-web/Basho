Good to know

Ordering meat in Jakarta

Brock is developed and operated by Basho Projects. It started as a solution to a problem in our own kitchens and became a business of its own.

How do I order from Brock?

Order direct on Tokopedia or Shopee and we deliver. No trade account or minimum order needed.

Do you supply restaurants and hotels?

Yes. We supply restaurants, hotels and hospitality groups across Jakarta and Indonesia with consistent cuts and spec. Message us on WhatsApp to set up trade supply.

Who is behind Brock?

Brock is run by Basho Projects, the team behind BEBOP, BRIQ and The Bluebell. Brock supplies BRIQ Bistro, so if the quality slips, we are the first to know.
