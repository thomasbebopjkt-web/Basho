The concept

Social. Modern. Soulful. BEBOP.

BEBOP is designed as a place to slow down. Music is treated as a central element rather than background noise, with vinyl selections and curated playlists setting the rhythm of the room throughout the night.

The cocktail programme is built on balance and clarity, favouring timeless recipes executed with intention. Drinks are designed to complement the space rather than dominate it, supporting long conversations and repeat visits.

Inspired by Japanese listening bars and classic cocktail culture, BEBOP focuses on restraint, detail and mood rather than volume or trend-driven theatrics.

Good to know

Where is BEBOP Bar + Kitchen?

BEBOP is at Jl. Senopati No. 61, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta — a few minutes’ walk from BRIQ Bistro and The Bluebell, and close to SCBD.

Is there live music at BEBOP?

Yes. Live jazz runs every Monday and Friday, curated by resident musician Bass G. Other nights bring resident DJs from the Bebop Music Collective, karaoke and trivia. Check Instagram for this week’s line-up.

What time does BEBOP open?

Every day from 18:00 until late.

Do I need to book a table?

Booking is the safest bet, especially for jazz nights and weekends. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll hold a table.

Is there food, or just drinks?

Both. The bar leads with cocktails, Mega Highballs and teapot cocktails, and the kitchen serves modern Japanese plates built for sharing.

Can I book BEBOP for a private event?

Yes. We host birthdays, launches, company nights and takeovers, with weekday and weekend free-flow packages for groups of 50 and up and minimum-spend options for smaller groups. Message us on WhatsApp for the 2026 packages.

Who are the DJs at BEBOP?

The Bebop Music Collective: 14 resident DJs who formed at BEBOP, playing house, future beats, jazz, soul, UK garage, disco and more.
