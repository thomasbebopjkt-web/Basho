Sunday Roast Comes to Senopati: The Bluebell’s Roast Beef, Roast Chicken and Nut Roast

3 November 2026 · Written by Thomas Brown

Roast beef, roast chicken or nut roast, with all the trimmings, every Sunday at Jakarta’s British pub.

Jakarta, 3 November 2026 — There are few things more British than a Sunday roast, and now there’s one on Jl. Tulodong Atas. The Bluebell Public House has launched its Sunday Roast: a proper plate of roast meat, Yorkshire pudding and gravy, served at a long table in the heart of Senopati.

For anyone searching for a British pub in Jakarta that does Sundays properly, this is the one. Start the day earlier with us, bring the family or the whole flat, and settle in. Our Sundays are pet friendly too, so the dog can come.

Pick your roast

Three roasts, each served with a Yorkshire pudding, cauliflower cheese, roast potatoes, roast carrots and cabbage, and a jug of gravy:

  • Roast Beef — 275K++
  • Roast Chicken — 200K++
  • Nut Roast — 150K++, our vegetarian roast

British classics all week

If Sunday isn’t your day, the kitchen serves British classics all week, from fish and chips to a proper pie. See the food at The Bluebell.

The Bluebell is open every day from 10:00, so on a Sunday in Senopati you can start with lunch and stay for the evening.

More at the pub this week

  • Pub Quiz, every Wednesday: teams, rounds and bragging rights.
  • The Chess Room: our snug, for a quiet corner and a game of chess.
  • Private dining room: seats twelve, for birthdays and family lunches. See the private rooms.
