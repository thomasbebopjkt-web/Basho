The pub

Draught beer. Ale. Whisky.

The Bluebell brings the social soul of the British pub to Senopati: a dimly lit, warm room where post-work drinks stretch into long, relaxed evenings. Thoughtfully elevated British classics from the kitchen; draught beer, ale and whisky at the bar, poured across six lines.

Cosy nooks, a proper bar lounge, a dining hall and a private dining room — approachably premium and easy to enjoy, whether you’re in for a quick bite after work or settling in for a nightcap.

Good to know

Where is The Bluebell Public House?

Jl. Tulodong Atas No. 10 A-B, Senopati, South Jakarta 12190 — between Senopati and SCBD, a short walk from BEBOP and BRIQ.

What are The Bluebell’s opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 10:00–01:00; Friday to Sunday 10:00–02:00.

What’s on tap?

Draught beer, ale and whisky lead the bar, poured across six draught lines, alongside fine spirits and signature cocktails.

Is it a proper British pub?

A modern one. The Bluebell is a modern British public house: elevated British classics from the kitchen, a bar lounge, a dining hall, a snug and a private dining room.

Can I book the private dining room?

Yes. The private dining room seats ten. Call or message us to book.
