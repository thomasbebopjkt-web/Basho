04 / FAQ

Investor questions

Projections and terms are only shared privately. Nothing on this page is an offer of investment.

Can I invest in a Basho Projects venue?

We bring partners in on new concepts rather than selling stakes in existing venues by default. Tell us what you’re looking for and we’ll tell you honestly whether there’s a fit.

What kind of projects do you open?

Bars, restaurants and food-led concepts with a clear point of view, mostly in and around Senopati and South Jakarta, where we know the streets, the landlords and the crowd.

Who runs the venue day to day?

We do. Every project is operated by the Basho team, with the same systems, suppliers and marketing engine behind BEBOP, BRIQ and The Bluebell.

Where can I see the numbers?

Projections, structure and terms are shared privately, project by project, once we’ve had a first conversation.

Let’s collaborate.

Whether you’re launching your first concept, reimagining an existing space, or looking for hands-on support across branding, operations, or experience design — we’d love to hear what you’re building.

We tailor each collaboration to fit the needs of the project and the people behind it.
