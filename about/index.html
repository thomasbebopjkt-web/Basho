About Basho Projects

Operators first. Everything else second.

We’re a collective of builders, operators and creatives behind some of Jakarta’s most distinct hospitality concepts.

We specialise in brands that feel lived-in and loved: naming and storytelling, service rituals, marketing plans and the operating systems underneath. Every member of the team has been in the trenches, launching and running venues from the ground up.

We run our own venues, and we take on a small number of partner projects each year, from full incubation to marketing alone. Together we turn big ideas into grounded realities that work — emotionally and commercially.

We’re not the biggest group in the city, and we’re fine with that. Small means the people who build a place are the same people you’ll find behind the bar on a Friday.

01 / How we got here

One bar, then a neighbourhood.

  1. BEBOP opens on Jl. SenopatiA bar first: cocktails, Mega Highballs and modern Japanese plates, with music at the centre of the room.
  2. 2022 · The DJs move inA group of DJs find a home at BEBOP and, the following year, launch the Bebop Music Collective — 14 residents strong today.
  3. BRIQ BistroA neighbourhood French bistro crafted between fine and casual, a few minutes’ walk away.
  4. 2026 · Brock ButcheryA problem in our own kitchens becomes a business: premium meat, priced honestly, delivered across Jakarta.
  5. September 2026 · The BluebellA modern British public house on Jl. Tulodong Atas, pouring across six draught lines.
  6. Bubble + Squeak MediaOur in-house agency for performance marketing, KOLs and PR, now open to other brands.
02 / What we believe
01

Test it on our own floor

Nothing we recommend is theory. If it hasn’t survived a full Saturday at one of our venues, we don’t sell it to you.

02

Sincerity over spectacle

Real food, real drinks, real conversations. Rooms people come back to on a Tuesday, not just photograph once.

03

Small roster, senior team

We take on a few partner projects a year so the people who pitched are the people who deliver.

04

Commercial, not corporate

We care about the numbers because we pay the rent too. We just don’t think a good night out should feel like a spreadsheet.
