Who we are

A collective of builders, operators, and creatives behind some of Jakarta’s most distinct hospitality concepts.

Basho Projects is a hospitality incubator, operator and consultancy based in Jakarta. We help founders, operators and brands bring concepts to market with clarity, speed and commercial discipline.

We run our own venues, which means every recommendation we make has already been tested against a roster, a rent cheque and a Saturday night service. We bring that same team to a small number of partner projects each year.

Some partners want the whole build. Others want us on marketing alone, or on one problem they cannot solve internally. Both work.

Our services

What we bring to your project

Go-to-Market Strategy

We define how a concept enters the market and how it wins once it is live. Positioning, audience definition, pricing strategy, launch planning, and channel selection — shaped by real operational insight in Jakarta and Southeast Asia. The playbook we used to open BRIQ.

Brand, Advertising and PR

Brands designed to perform in the real world, not just online. Brand strategy, identity, messaging, content direction, advertising, and public relations — built for launch, growth, and consistency. Run in-house by Bubble + Squeak Media.

Hospitality Concept Development

Restaurants, bars, cafés, and food-led retail developed from the ground up: concept definition, menu direction, service experience, layout logic, and supplier integration. BEBOP went from idea to open under this process.
