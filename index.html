03 / Why we’re different

We’re not the biggest group in Jakarta. That’s the point.

Basho Projects is a hospitality incubator, operator and consultancy. Every recommendation we make has already been tested against a roster, a rent cheque and a Saturday night service — on our own floors, with our own money.

That keeps us honest. It also keeps us small on purpose: a handful of venues we run properly, and a few partner projects a year where the senior team actually shows up.

07 / Good to know

Questions people ask us

What is Basho Projects?

Basho Projects is an independent hospitality group in Senopati, South Jakarta. We own and run BEBOP Bar + Kitchen, BRIQ Bistro, The Bluebell Public House and Brock Butchery, and we help other founders and investors build bars and restaurants of their own.

Where are your venues?

All within a few minutes’ walk of each other in Senopati, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta: BEBOP on Jl. Senopati, BRIQ on Jl. Prof. Joko Sutono and The Bluebell on Jl. Tulodong Atas.

Can Basho Projects help with my restaurant or bar?

Yes. We take on a small number of partner projects each year, from full incubation to a single problem like a menu, a launch or a team that isn’t clicking. See our services or get in touch.

Can I invest in a Basho Projects venue?

We bring in partners on new concepts. Tell us a little about yourself on the invest page and we’ll set up a conversation.

Let’s collaborate.

Whether you’re launching your first concept, reimagining an existing space, or looking for hands-on support across branding, operations, or experience design — we’d love to hear what you’re building.

We tailor each collaboration to fit the needs of the project and the people behind it.

Let’s talk — coffee, cocktails, or call.
