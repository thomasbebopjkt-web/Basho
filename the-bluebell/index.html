The Bluebell Public House

A modern British public house and chophouse in Senopati, South Jakarta.

SocialInstagram →Find usGoogle Maps →Call0821-4401-216 →ReadOpening story →

Draught beer. Ale. Whisky.

The Bluebell brings the social soul of the British pub to Senopati: a dimly lit, warm room where post-work drinks stretch into long, relaxed evenings. Thoughtfully elevated British classics from the kitchen; draught beer, ale and whisky at the bar, poured across six lines.

Cosy nooks, a proper bar lounge, a dining hall and a private dining room — approachably premium and easy to enjoy, whether you’re in for a quick bite after work or settling in for a nightcap.

  • AddressJl. Tulodong Atas No. 10 A-B, Senopati, South Jakarta 12190
  • Hours10:00–01:00 Monday to Thursday · 10:00–02:00 Friday to Sunday

The Bluebell is developed and operated by Basho Projects.
