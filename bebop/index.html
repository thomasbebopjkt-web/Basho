BEBOP Bar + Kitchen

An intimate listening bar and cocktail concept, where sound, cocktails and atmosphere work together as one experience.

ReservationsBook now →MenuFood + drinks →SocialInstagram →Find usGoogle Maps →

Social. Modern. Soulful. BEBOP.

BEBOP is designed as a place to slow down. Music is treated as a central element rather than background noise, with vinyl selections and curated playlists setting the rhythm of the room throughout the night.

The cocktail programme is built on balance and clarity, favouring timeless recipes executed with intention. Drinks are designed to complement the space rather than dominate it, supporting long conversations and repeat visits.

Inspired by Japanese listening bars and classic cocktail culture, BEBOP focuses on restraint, detail and mood rather than volume or trend-driven theatrics — carefully curated music, considered lighting, and a service style that values presence and precision.

Live jazz runs every Monday and Friday. Read about Jazz at BEBOP →

BEBOP Bar + Kitchen is developed and operated by Basho Projects.
