The concept

Organic. Human. Eclectic. Raw. Fine-casual.

BRIQ Bistro is designed around authentic culinary traditions presented with clarity and restraint: quality ingredients, classic techniques, and balanced presentation. The dining experience feels both refined and approachable — casual enough for a relaxed brunch, polished enough for a memorable dinner.

BRIQ was created for Jakarta’s growing dining audience: diners who appreciate quality behind the plate, a warm room, and genuine hospitality.

  • Refined yet approachable French cooking in a bistro setting
  • Positioned between fine dining and casual dining
  • An approachable wine list and an outdoor terrace
  • Meat from our own butcher, Brock
The space

Casual, but never careless.

An intimate, sunlit room by day and a cosy, low-lit bistro by night. Open shelves, textured walls, standing tables that invite you to linger and an outdoor terrace for a quick drink or a cigarette between courses.

We don’t chase trends. We build lasting tables for good conversations — lunch, dinner, drinks, or just a place to catch your breath in Senopati.

The signature

Home of the steak frites.

If you only order one thing at BRIQ, make it this: a properly seared steak, crisp frites and a sauce worth mopping up. It’s the dish we’re known for in Senopati, made with beef from our own butcher, Brock.

Alongside it, the kitchen runs rotating pasta promos and monthly specials. Follow @briqbistro for this week’s offer.

Good to know

Where is BRIQ Bistro?

Jl. Prof. Joko Sutono SH No. 7B, Kebayoran Baru, South Jakarta — in the heart of Senopati, a short walk from BEBOP and The Bluebell.

What are BRIQ’s opening hours?

Lunch and brunch from 10:00 to 16:00, dinner from 17:00 to 22:00.

What kind of food does BRIQ serve?

Soulful French bistro cooking, and we’re best known for our steak frites. The kitchen also runs rotating pasta promos and monthly specials, so check Instagram for what’s on this week.

Is there outdoor seating?

Yes, BRIQ has an outdoor terrace as well as the dining room.

How do I book a table at BRIQ?

Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm your table.
