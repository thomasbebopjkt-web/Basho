BRIQ Bistro

Crafted between fine and casual. The precision and technique of fine dining with the warmth and ease of a neighbourhood bistro.

Organic. Human. Eclectic. Raw. Fine-casual.

BRIQ is designed around authentic culinary traditions presented with clarity and restraint: quality ingredients, classic techniques, and balanced presentation. Casual enough for a relaxed brunch, polished enough for a memorable dinner.

Created for Jakarta’s growing dining audience — diners who appreciate quality behind the plate, a warm room, and genuine hospitality.

  • Refined yet approachable French cooking in a bistro setting
  • Positioned between fine dining and casual dining
  • Design that balances comfort with elevated details
  • Consistency backed by Basho Projects’ operating systems
