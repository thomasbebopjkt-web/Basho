03 / How it works
  1. Listen

    A coffee, a cocktail or a call. We learn the concept, the site, the budget and what you actually want out of it.

  2. Test

    We pressure-test the idea against what we see on our own floors every night, and tell you straight if it won’t work.

  3. Build

    Brand, menu, bar, team, systems and numbers, built alongside you with no black boxes or surprises.

  4. Launch & run

    Opening, marketing and the first months of trading, until it runs without us.

06 / FAQ

Before you ask

What does a hospitality consultant in Jakarta actually do?

We help you decide what to open, where, and for whom; then we build the brand, menu, bar programme, team and systems, and launch it. You can hire us for all of it or one piece.

Do you only work on new openings?

No. Some of our best work is on venues that are already open: a menu that isn’t selling, a launch that didn’t land, a team that keeps turning over, or marketing that isn’t filling tables.

How much does it cost?

Monthly retainers start from IDR 20 million. For the right project we also work on a revenue-partner model, so our fee moves with your results. Bubble + Squeak marketing packages start from USD 950 a month.

Do you work outside Jakarta?

Our home is Senopati, but we work with brands across Indonesia and Southeast Asia where the project is a good fit.

Who will actually work on my project?

The same senior team that runs BEBOP, BRIQ, The Bluebell and Brock. That’s why we only take on a few projects a year.

Whether you’re launching your first concept, reimagining an existing space, or looking for hands-on support across branding, operations, or experience design — we’d love to hear what you’re building.
